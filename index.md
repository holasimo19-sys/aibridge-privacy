# AI Bridge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how the AI Bridge Instagram account [@aibridge.tools](https://www.instagram.com/aibridge.tools/) and its private publishing tool, "AIbridg", handle information. The tool publishes our own reels and answers people who ask for a link in the comments.

## What we collect

When you comment on one of our reels, Instagram shares the following with our tool through Meta's official Instagram API: your Instagram user ID and username, and the text of your comment and when you posted it.

If your comment contains the keyword shown in the reel, we also record that we replied to you and when. We do not collect your email, phone number, location, contacts, or any data from outside our own posts.

## How we use it

We use it only to send you one private message with the link you asked for, in reply to your comment; to make sure nobody gets more than one message per reel, or more than one message a day; and to count, in total, how many people asked for each link, so we know which videos are useful.

We never send messages to people who did not comment the keyword, and we never sell, rent or share your information with anyone. We do not use it for advertising.

## Where it is stored and for how long

The information is stored only on the account owner's own computer, not on a shared server. We keep comment records only so the "one message per person" rule keeps working, and delete them whenever you ask (see below).

## Links in our messages

Some links we send are affiliate links, and the message always says so. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission. The website you visit has its own privacy policy.

## Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete everything we hold about you at any time. Send a direct message to [@aibridge.tools](https://www.instagram.com/aibridge.tools/) on Instagram with the words "delete my data". We delete your comment records and message log within 30 days and confirm when it is done. Deleting your own comment on Instagram also stops us from contacting you about it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct or delete your data, or to object to how we use it. Contact us using the details above and we will help.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: send a direct message to [@aibridge.tools](https://www.instagram.com/aibridge.tools/) on Instagram.
